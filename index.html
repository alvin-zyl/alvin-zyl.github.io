About

I work on making LLM pre-training cheaper, faster, and more reliable.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of California, Santa Barbara, advised by Prof. Zheng Zhang. Before my Ph.D., I received an M.A. in Statistics from UCSB and a B.S. in Statistics from Nankai University.

My research covers the pre-training stack end to end: low-rank model architectures that reduce compute, Muon-style optimizers that train faster, and scalable, fault-tolerant training systems designed for clusters of 100k+ GPUs.

Most recently, I was a Software Engineer Intern at Google Cloud. Before that, I was a visiting student at Argonne National Laboratory and a research intern at Cadence Design Systems.

I am on the job market for full-time industry research and engineering roles starting in 2027. Feel free to reach out at ziyueliu@ucsb.edu.

Research

My current research is on efficient LLM pre-training, across three layers of the training stack. Earlier in my graduate studies, I worked on scientific machine learning, building efficient neural PDE solvers with applications in 3D-IC thermal design.

LLM pre-training stack

Architecture

Low-rank and tensor-compressed models that cut the compute and memory cost of pre-training while preserving quality.

CoLA EMNLP'25 LaX NeurIPS'25 CoMERA NeurIPS'24

Optimizer

Muon-based optimizers that further speed up convergence, reduce orthogonalization cost, and improve quality.

Muon² EMNLP'26 MuonQ COLM'26 Muon+ Under review TEON Under review

System

Scalable training for low-rank models, and fault-tolerant pre-training that avoids system stalling on frequent restarts at O(100k+) scale.

ReCoVer NeurIPS'26 BOOST MLSys'26

Scientific machine learning

PDE solvers

Physics-informed neural networks and neural operators for fast PDE solving, applied to 3D-IC thermal simulation and design.
