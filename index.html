About

I work on making LLM training cheaper, faster, and more reliable.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of California, Santa Barbara, advised by Prof. Zheng Zhang. Before my Ph.D., I received an M.A. in Statistics from UCSB and a B.S. in Statistics from Nankai University.

My research covers the training stack end to end, particularly for pre-training: low-rank model architectures that reduce compute, Muon-style optimizers that train faster, and scalable, fault-tolerant training systems designed for clusters of 100k+ GPUs.

I also work on AI for EDA and data center thermal management, building ML surrogate models that speed up thermal and CFD simulation, from 3D-IC chips to data center digital twins.

Most recently, I was a Software Engineer Intern at Google Cloud AI. Before that, I was a visiting student at Argonne National Laboratory and a research intern at Cadence Design Systems.

I am on the job market for full-time industry research and engineering roles starting in 2027. Feel free to reach out at ziyueliu@ucsb.edu.

Research

My main research is on efficient LLM training, across three layers of the stack. My second line of work is AI for EDA and data center thermal management, built up through papers and three research internships at Cadence.

LLM training stack

Architecture

Low-rank and tensor-compressed models that cut the compute and memory cost of training while preserving quality.

CoLA EMNLP'25Oral LaX NeurIPS'25 CoMERA NeurIPS'24

Optimizer

Muon-based optimizers that further speed up convergence, reduce orthogonalization cost, and improve quality.

Muon² EMNLP'26Oral MuonQ COLM'26 Muon+ Under review TEON Under review

System

Scalable training for low-rank models, and fault-tolerant training that avoids system stalling on frequent restarts at O(100k+) scale.

ReCoVer NeurIPS'26 BOOST MLSys'26

AI for EDA / Data center

Chip

Physics-driven operator learning for 3D-IC thermal design that speeds up thermal simulation by orders of magnitude, handles multi-scale and multi-die designs, and enables fast, trustworthy thermal optimization.

DeepOHeat DAC'23 DeepOHeat-v1 TCPMT'26 DeepOHeat-v2 Under review Cadence internship 2022

Data center

AI surrogates for data center digital twins, trained on data from physics-based solvers and integrated with Cadence's Reality Digital Twin Platform to predict airflow and server temperatures in near real time.

Cadence internship 2024

Methods

Faster and lighter physics-informed networks and neural operators, and CFD surrogates that scale to large automotive aerodynamic simulations.
